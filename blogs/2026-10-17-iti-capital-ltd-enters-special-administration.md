On 25 September 2026, ITI Capital Ltd (ITI Capital) entered special administration. Duncan Perring and David Soden, both of Teneo Financial Advisory Ltd (Teneo), were appointed as special administrators. ITI Capital is a FCA authorised and regulated brokerage company that helped customers invest in shares and bonds and looked after their investments.On 10 August 2025, ITI Capital agreed to stop carrying out most regulated activity (UK and overseas) and to stop accepting any new client money or custody assets.
